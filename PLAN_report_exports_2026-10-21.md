## 1. Decisions taken on the owner's behalf

All of these are chat confirmation only, NOT sign off. Each says what was decided, why, what it costs and how to change it.

### D1. What counts as a report
- Decision: a "report" is a rendered, human read document or workbook that summarises results (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, HTML print view).
  CSV and JSON are "data exports": layout is left alone, only accuracy, scope, filename and formula injection safety are fixed.
  Uploaded originals (evidence, policies, templates) are "file downloads", not reports.
- Why: a cover and footer inside a CSV or JSON breaks the parsers that read them.
- Cost: data exports get no graphs. The Reports page also offers its CSV so the figures stay machine readable.
- Change: move an item between the tables in section 2.

### D2. Customer branded and frozen documents are out of the ThemisIQ theme
- Decision: ARIA policy documents (customer template branding engine, policy publication) and the AI Governance response letter
  (frozen, hashed, "no more in it than the JSON has") keep their own look. They are listed as documents, not reports.
- Why: the first carries the customer's brand by design; the second is a hashed letter to an outside requester.
- Cost: two outputs do not carry the silver cover. Both stay consistent with their own approval and hash rules.
- Change: render them through `core/report_kit` and re-baseline the hash tests.

### D3. Report tier: one explicit attribute, `tier`
- Decision: every report definition carries `tier` = `board` or `operational` (module `core/report_kit/model.py`).
  Board: short (cover, one page of headline measures, 2 to 4 charts, top items, "decisions needed", limits), summary first, no record
  lists beyond the top ten, a dark gunmetal cover and the label "BOARD REPORT". Operational: cover, charts, full tables, supporting
  detail, a light silver cover and the label "OPERATIONAL REPORT". The label is on the cover and in the footer.
- Why: the owner asked for a real, visible distinction.
- Cost: board layouts omit lists; operational ones are longer.
- Change: `TIERS` and the per type `tier` in `modules/launcher/report_documents.py`.

### D4. Palette (one place: `core/report_kit/theme.py`)
- Decision: a silver and graphite family with depth. Graphite is for text, silver is for fills, rules and bands.
  `ink #1F2328`, `graphite #2E3338`, `gunmetal #3A4047`, `slate #5C646C`, `silver_deep #8A9199`, `silver #B8BEC6`,
  `silver_light #D5D9DE`, `silver_mist #E8EBEE`, `pearl #F4F6F8`. Semantic: `bad #A32A22`, `warn #9A5B00`, `good #2E7D32`.
  Chart series run graphite to silver by lightness, with value labels, so a black and white print still reads.
- Why: silver is not a text colour on white, so text stays graphite (contrast above 7 to 1); fills use the silver ramp.
- Cost: no blues anywhere in generated documents (the logo keeps its own navy and gold).
- Change: edit the constants in `theme.py`; every renderer reads them.

### D5. Footer wording and time
- Decision: `Module: <nav name> | Generated by <full name> (<username>) | Generated <date time> UTC (<time> CAT) | <tier label> | Page n of N`
  (the PDF sets it on two lines at the foot of every page, including the cover; Word, Excel and the print view carry the same words).
  Scheduled or system runs read `Scheduled run for <owner full name> (<username>)` and `System run for <owner ...>`.
  Module names follow the platform navigation (`base_shell.html`): Governance (aria), Audit (grid), Resilience (bcm),
  Privacy (sentinel), Enterprise Risk (erm), Operations Risk (orm), AI Governance, Command Centre, Evidence Vault.
  The app stores naive UTC; UTC is shown first as the canonical value and CAT (fixed UTC+2, no DST) beside it.
- Why: the owner reads CAT; a bare UTC stamp invites mistakes. CAT needs no tz database, so it renders on Windows and Ubuntu alike.
- Cost: one longer footer line. Change: `theme.DISPLAY_ZONE` and `identity.MODULE_NAV`.

### D6. Chart engine and dependencies
- Decision: no new dependency. Charts are reportlab `Drawing` scenes (vector in PDF), a small SVG emitter over the same scene for HTML, a small
  Pillow rasteriser for DOCX images, openpyxl native charts in XLSX. Fonts: Helvetica in PDF, Vera (bundled with reportlab) for PNG.
- Why: reportlab 4.1, Pillow (via qrcode[pil]), python-docx and openpyxl are already pinned in `requirements.txt`.
  `renderPM` needs rlPyCairo, which is not installed, so it is not used.
- Cost: only the chart types in `charts.py` exist (horizontal bar, stacked bar, donut, line).

### D7. Board reports that do not exist yet
- Decision: build no large new report types. Board tier is assigned to the existing board style outputs (Executive Brief,
  Executive Summary, Board report preset, ERM, ORM and BCM "Board Report" panels). Missing board variants are listed in section 3.
- Exception recorded here: Sentinel "RoPA Report", "Breach Report" and "Compliance Score" buttons are labelled reports but return a
  ZIP of raw JSON. They become real PDFs on the shared kit (small, data already computed). The ZIP evidence pack stays.

### D8. Audit report: one definition, two tiers
- Decision: the GRID audit report is one report with a `tier` parameter. Operational is the full audit report (every control, every
  finding, management responses). Board (`?tier=board`) is the short summary of that one audit: headline measures, charts, the
  top findings and what needs a decision. It is not a cross audit portfolio report (the Reports page "Audit status" covers that).
- Why: the owner asked for a real board versus normal distinction; two figure computations would drift, so both tiers read one.
- Cost: no portfolio board report across audits in the Audit module itself. Change: `report_service.build_report` and `?tier`.

### D9. Privacy compliance score: a part with nothing to measure is "not measured"
- Decision: a part with nothing recorded (no breach, no request, no vendor, no high risk record, no RoPA record) has no score
  (`null`), takes no share of the overall, and the weights of the measured parts are scaled to 100. When no part can be measured the
  overall is `null`, shown as "Not measured", grade "N/A". Percentages round half up. An emptied data category list (`[]`) no longer
  counts as a category.
- Why: the old score read 100 for each empty part and 0 for an empty RoPA, so a module holding nothing read as 75, a B.
  "Nothing recorded" is not "100% compliant", and the owner asked that figures match the real data.
- Cost: `/sentinel/api/compliance-score` can now return `null` for `overall` and for a part; a module with a single closed breach
  reads 100 until more is recorded. Anything that parses the JSON must accept `null`.
- Change: `data_service.get_compliance_score` (return 100 for an empty part to restore the old reading) and `SCORE_WEIGHTS`.

### D10. Privacy evidence pack: each part needs the permission of its own list
- Decision: the ZIP (`/sentinel/api/audit-export`) includes a part only when the caller holds the capability that opens the same
  records on screen (breach log `sentinel.breach.manage`, request log `sentinel.dsr.manage`, impact assessments
  `sentinel.dpia.manage`, vendors `sentinel.vendor.manage`). A part left out is named in the README. Asking for `type=breach` alone
  without the capability is a 403. The RoPA, promised by the route's own description and never in the pack, is added. Every part
  is complete (the screens stop at 500 rows). Every export is written to the audit trail.
- Why: any role with module access (compliance manager, privacy analyst) could download the breach log, the request log with
  requester names and addresses and the vendor register through the pack, although the lists themselves answer 403.
- Cost: those roles get a smaller pack. Change: the `parts` table in `api_audit_export` (`modules/sentinel/routes.py`).

### D11. Record level documents (DPIA, plan, AI impact assessment) are previews, not features (2026-10-22)
- Decision: when the owner asked to see an exported RoPA, DPIA, BCP and AIIA, only the RoPA register was a real export. The other three were rendered as
  previews through the report kit from each module's own records (`get_dpia`, `get_plan` with its coverage links, reviews and activations, `get_assessment`)
  and are NOT wired to a route or a button. The builders are kept in the shared notes folder (`report-samples/record-documents/builders`), not in the repo.
- Why: a record level export is a permission, unit scope and audit decision (who may download a DPIA that names data subjects, whether a plan needs the same
  gate as the module, whether an assessment that a person may not open is named), and the look had not been approved. Unwired code in the repo would be dead code.
- Cost: until they are wired, a DPIA still downloads as plain text, and a plan and an assessment cannot be exported.
- Change: wire each builder behind the capability that opens the same record (`sentinel.dpia.manage`, `module.bcm.access`, `ai_assessment.view`), through the
  record's own unit scope (404 for another unit's record), write the download to the audit trail, add a button beside the record, and test it with known data.

## 3. Gaps (board level or expected report missing)

Listed, not built (D7: no large new report types without a ledger entry). Each is a candidate for the owner to rank.

- G1 Scheduled or emailed reports do not exist. The report definition form kept a Schedule control that stored a value and ran nothing; it was
  removed. The kit already attributes a run to its owner (`Actor(kind="scheduled")`, footer "Scheduled run for <owner> (<username>)"), so a
  scheduler can be added without touching the documents.
- G2 Enterprise Risk Scenario Studio board packs are hashed snapshots shown as cards. They have no printable or downloadable document.
- G3 Resilience exercise after action report is JSON only (`/bcm/api/exercises/{id}/report`). A PDF of the frozen snapshot is the missing document.
- G4 Privacy has no standalone board version of the breach register or the RoPA. The compliance score is the privacy board report and
  carries the headline breach, request, DPIA and vendor counts.
- G5 Evidence Vault, Data Readiness and AI Governance have exports (CSV, files, signed JSON) and no summary report, so no board variant.
- G6 The module board reports and the Reports page documents are offered as PDF and Excel (and a print view on the Reports page). Word is
  offered only for the audit report.
- G7 Documents generated before this work (saved audit reports, R07) keep their old look.
- G8 Record level documents. A single DPIA, business continuity plan or AI impact assessment has no document export. The DPIA viewer offers a plain
  text download of the AI draft (X21, no cover, no footer, no figures); a plan can only be read on its page; an AI impact assessment can only be read on
  its page (AI Governance exports JSON packs and signed decision records, which are not these). Previews on the report kit are in section 8. None is wired to a
  route or a button, because wiring is a permission, scope and audit decision for the owner (the RoPA register is the one record export that exists).
- G9 Linking to other documentation (owner instruction of 2026-10-22, section 9): the record documents session adds link support and the Evidence Vault section; the Case report and the Audit report
  adopt it in a patch. The registers, the board reports and the other Reports page types do not link yet, and Operations Risk records cannot carry Vault files (not Vault link targets).
