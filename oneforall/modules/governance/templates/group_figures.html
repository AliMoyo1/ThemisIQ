Group figures

Frozen figures each organization publishes about itself, as of the time shown. A subsidiary that has not published recently shows as not reporting. It is never counted as zero. Counts are added up for the group; shares and levels are shown per organization because they cannot be added.

Corrections to your published figures

A correction is proposed by one person and approved by another. Approval publishes a new figure marked as restated; the original stays on record.
