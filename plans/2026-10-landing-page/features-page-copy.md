## What sets ThemisIQ apart

**Connected, not bolted together.** A confirmed breach can open work in risk, operations and audit, and every team sees
its own next step from the same event.

**One control, many standards.** Map equivalent controls across standards once, then audit them side by side in one
integrated audit, with the same evidence.

**Rules for every jurisdiction, approved by people.** Each privacy rule is drafted by a specialist and approved by a
different DPO before it sets a deadline.

**A second pair of eyes, built in.** Decisions, control matches, rule cards and policies ask someone other than the
author to approve them.

**AI you can govern.** Every assistant follows your organisation's AI policy, shows its sources when it answers from
your records, and leaves the decision to a person.

## AI across the platform

### An assistant in every module. Working from your records. Governed by your policy.

ThemisIQ's assistants draft, analyse and answer inside the work itself, so the first version is never a blank page.
People review, decide and approve.

[Showcase] Tabs for ARIA, Sentinel, BCM, ERM, GRID and AI Governance. Each shows one assistant card with a sample
request, a short sample answer, a source line and the label "Human review required".

**What the assistants do**

- **Governance:** write a policy, procedure, standard, guideline or framework for a control, or one integrated document
  across standards; run a gap analysis; answer questions from your approved policies with sources.
- **Privacy:** draft a full DPIA, a privacy notice and a reply to a subject request; assess a breach; suggest a legal
  basis and the risks of an activity; compare two privacy regimes.
- **Resilience:** draft a continuity plan from a scenario, review a plan, suggest incident actions and answer from your
  continuity documents.
- **Enterprise risk:** score risks, suggest treatments, write risk statements, build assessment questions, and scan the
  horizon from trusted sources with citations checked.
- **Audit:** read an imported checklist, suggest control details, score audit items and write the report narrative.
- **Operations risk:** analyse an event and explain the trend.
- **AI governance:** explain an AI use in plain words for its reviewers.
- **Evidence:** find evidence by what it says and suggest where it belongs.
- **Leadership:** a morning briefing, an executive summary and predictive risk across every module.

**The guardrails**

- **Your policy decides.** Choose the AI providers, the kinds of data that may be sent, the features that are on and a
  monthly budget. A refused request never leaves the platform.
- **Sources, not guesses.** Answers from your records show where they came from, and external sources are checked
  before they are cited.
- **People decide.** A draft is a draft. Approvals, decisions and publication are always a person's act.
- **Everything on record.** Every AI request is logged with its feature, outcome and usage, and every AI feature is
  documented: what it reads, what it leaves out and what happens to its answer.
- **Your access, respected.** Suggestions only name records you are allowed to open.
- **Honest when unavailable.** If the assistant is off or cannot answer, nothing is made up and you carry on by hand.

Small print: "AI features need an AI provider to be configured and follow your organisation's AI policy."

## Governance and controls · ARIA

### Give every control its context, across every standard you follow.

#### Policy authoring studio

[Showcase] A five step study with a realistic document.

1. **Generate.** "Pick a control and a document type. ARIA writes a complete first draft in the voice the standard
   expects. Add your own instructions, or write one integrated document for the control and its equivalents in other
   standards."
2. **Edit.** "Work on the draft in the Policy Workbench. Save as you go, discard a draft or bring it back. Every author is
   recorded."
3. **Preview in your template.** "See the finished document in your own branded template as a PDF before anything is
   official."
4. **Approve.** "Send the version to an approver. The author and the person who submitted it can never approve it.
   Compare any two versions line by line."
5. **Publish.** "The approved version is filed in the Evidence Vault and attached as evidence to the matching audit
   controls. Start a revision any time without touching what was approved."

Footnote: "Illustrative example. Every draft is reviewed and approved by people before it is published."
Setup: "The authoring workflow is switched on for each organisation."

jumps to Audit.

Footnote: "Illustrative example. Matches in your organisation are proposed from your frameworks and approved by your
reviewers."

**Frameworks and controls**
- Load standards from the catalogue and manage their controls.
- See each control's integrated status across your standards.
- Attach proof from the Evidence Vault to the control it supports.

**Integrated management system**
- Propose equivalent controls across standards: grouped by topic, compared by wording, and unclear pairs checked by the

- A different reviewer approves each proposed match.

**Policies and documents**
- Generate, edit, preview, approve and publish policies with full version history.
- Upload your company templates and apply your branding to policy documents.
- Compare any two retained versions line by line.

**Ask and analyse**
- Ask ARIA: answers from your approved policies and controls, with the source shown.
- An assisted gap analysis of a framework.

**Oversight**
- A governance risk list tied to controls and policies.
- An activity log of control, policy and document changes.
- Compliance projects per framework.

AI: drafting, integrated documents, gap analysis, Ask ARIA and control matching.
Works with: Audit, Evidence Vault, Enterprise risk, AI Governance.

## AI Governance

### Know every AI use, decide on it with evidence, and keep watching it.

For your governance, risk, privacy and compliance specialists.

[Showcase] The life of one AI use, in six panels:

1. **Register.** "Describe the AI use in a guided intake. It stays a draft until you submit it."
2. **Review.** "A reviewer accepts it into the inventory. Its tier follows the answers, and a reviewer can adopt a
   different tier with a reason."
3. **Assess.** "Impact assessments, prefilled from the use, with a checklist of what is left before a decision."
4. **Decide.** "Someone other than the person who asked approves, sets conditions, returns or rejects, on a frozen record
   of what they were shown."
5. **Watch.** "Evaluations, human oversight reviewed by a second person, and regulatory changes checked against
   affected uses."
6. **Prove.** "Review packs for auditors and a full history."

**Inventory**
- An AI systems inventory with a guided intake, saved as a draft until submitted.
- Bulk import of AI uses from a form export.
- A reviewer accepts each use and can adopt a different tier with a reason.

**Assess and decide**
- AI impact assessments with configurable dimensions, prefilled from the AI use.
- A checklist of which assessments apply and what is left before a decision.
- Decisions by someone other than the requester, signed on a frozen record.
- Conditions with expiry, and a re-review when a covered fact changes.

**Watch**
- Monitoring and evaluations.
- Human oversight arrangements, reviewed by a second person.
- Regulatory changes checked against affected AI uses.

**Proof**
- Review packs for auditors.
- A register of ThemisIQ's own AI features and how they are used.

AI: plain-language explanations of each AI use for its reviewers.
Setup: "AI Governance is switched on for each organisation."
Works with: Privacy, Operations risk, Governance, Audit, Evidence Vault.
