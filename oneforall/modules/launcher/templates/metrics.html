How each figure is defined What a number counts, what it is a share of, which rows are left out, and where its rows are listed.

Every figure on a dashboard is read through your own access. A figure is shown only when it was measured: when nothing is recorded to measure it against it says so, and when it could not be read it is blank, never a zero. Only figures from modules you may open are listed here.
