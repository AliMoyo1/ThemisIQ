# Operational assurance closeout, 24 October 2026

## What the application now does

- Themis reads one selected, authorized record and a bounded first hop of confirmed links, Vault proof metadata, or change signals. Each answer names its source records with numbered links. Empty or unavailable evidence produces an explicit inability to verify. The selected page is only a candidate ID: module access and business-unit scope are checked again on every read. Themis still does not read file contents or draft through an external AI provider.
- New frozen report runs receive a tenant-scoped retention date. The initial product setting is 2,555 days (seven 365-day years). An Organization Administrator, Compliance Manager, Chief GRC Officer, or Super Admin may change it from 30 to 3,650 days on the Reports page, with an audit reason. The same report-retention capability governs a single run's date or hold. This changes **new runs only**. Existing run dates, holds, and sign-offs are unaffected. This starting period is not a universal statutory period; each client must approve its own schedule, including records subject to litigation hold, financial regulation, or sector-specific retention.
- Saved reports retain independent sign-off: a different person with `platform.report.signoff` (Compliance Manager, Chief GRC Officer, or Super Admin) must review an intact snapshot, give a reason, and expressly review any AI narrative. The author cannot sign their own run. Shares remain time-limited and recheck current scope on open.
- The email configuration, secret-free health view, outbox, retry controls, and template path already exist. Domain, monitored Reply-To, credentials, DNS authentication, delivery tests, and live sender cutover are postponed at the owner's request. Console/log-only status is not delivery.

## Themis provider-assisted drafting gate

Selected-record answers and drafts remain local. Enabling an OpenRouter call for connected-record text requires a separate privacy assessment, an approved data-category policy per organization, provider/model routing restrictions, verified retention terms, prompt minimization, a citation checker that rejects unsupported claims, and a human review step before any external response. [OpenRouter's privacy policy](https://openrouter.ai/privacy) says routed model providers have differing retention and training practices. A routing setting alone is insufficient evidence that every downstream provider meets a client's policy. Until the review is approved, keep connected-record text out of provider requests.
