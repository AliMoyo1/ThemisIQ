Guardrails built in

Grounded or silentAnswers only from your approved records.

Ask ARIA answers from your approved policies, controls and risks, never from outside knowledge. If they do not cover the question, it says so and names the owner to ask. If an assistant is off or cannot answer, nothing is made up.

Fact kept apart from inferenceNever invents a clause, source or link.

Every assistant separates what your records support from inference and says what a person should verify. A cut-off or unreadable answer is rejected, never shown or saved as if it were complete.

Trusted citations onlyWeb research cites your trusted sites.

Horizon scanning and research cite only domains on your trusted list. Anything else is dropped before you see it.

A pinned modelA different model is rejected as drift.

The exact model is fixed. If a provider answers with a different one, the answer is rejected. AI uses are evaluated against thresholds, and an approval is re-reviewed when a fact it relied on changes.

Your policy decidesProviders, data, features and budget.

Choose the AI providers, the kinds of data that may be sent, the features that are on and a monthly budget. A refused request never leaves the platform. Through the AI gateway, requests go only to providers that keep nothing and collect nothing from your data.

People decide, on the recordDrafts stay drafts until a person acts.

Approvals, decisions and publication are always a person's act. Every AI request is logged with its feature and outcome, and suggestions only name records you are allowed to open.

AI assistants and guardrails

What the assistants do

  • Governance: write a policy, procedure, standard, guideline or framework for a control, or one integrated document across standards; run a gap analysis; answer from your approved policies with sources.
  • Privacy: draft a full DPIA, a privacy notice and a reply to a subject request; assess a breach; suggest a legal basis and the risks of an activity; compare two privacy regimes.
  • Resilience: draft a continuity plan from a scenario, review a plan, suggest incident actions and answer from your continuity documents.
  • Enterprise risk: score risks, suggest treatments, write risk statements, build assessment questions, and scan the horizon from trusted sources with citations checked.
  • Audit: read an imported checklist, suggest control details, score audit items and write the report narrative.
  • Operations risk: analyse an event and explain the trend.
  • AI Governance: explain an AI use in plain words for its reviewers.
  • Evidence: find evidence by what it says and suggest where it belongs.
  • Leadership: a morning briefing, an executive summary and predictive risk across every module.

Guardrails against hallucination

  • Grounded or silent. Ask ARIA answers only from your approved policies, controls and risks, never from outside knowledge. If they do not cover the question, it says so and names the owner to ask.
  • Fact kept apart from inference. Every assistant is instructed to separate what your records support from inference, never to invent a clause, source or link, and to say what a person should verify.
  • Citations from trusted sources only. Web research cites only domains on your trusted list; anything else is dropped before you see it.
  • Incomplete answers refused. A cut-off or unreadable answer is rejected, never shown or saved as if it were complete.
  • Honest when unavailable. If the assistant is off or cannot answer, nothing is made up and you carry on by hand.

Guardrails against drift

  • A pinned model. The exact model is fixed. If a provider answers with a different one, the answer is rejected as drift.
  • Watched over time. AI uses are evaluated against thresholds, and an approval is re-reviewed when a fact it relied on changes.
  • People decide. A draft is a draft. Approvals, decisions and publication are always a person's act.

Guardrails for control and privacy

  • Your policy decides. Choose the AI providers, the kinds of data that may be sent, the features that are on and a monthly budget. A refused request never leaves the platform.
  • No retention, no training. Through the AI gateway, requests go only to providers that keep nothing and collect nothing from your data.
  • Everything on record. Every AI request is logged with its feature, outcome and usage, and every AI feature is documented: what it reads, what it leaves out and what happens to its answer.
  • Your access, respected. Suggestions only name records you are allowed to open.
